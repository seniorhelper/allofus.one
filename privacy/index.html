Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026.

Short version: your LIFEboard is private to you, your selfie face is only shared if you are signed in and choose to use it on your avatar, and we never sell your information.

What we collect

Account: email, password (handled by our login provider, Supabase), display name, username, city if you add it, and your date of birth (to keep allofus.one adults-only; it is private and never shown on your profile). Public profile: your name, status ring, life paths, causes, bio, avatar look and, if you add one, a small copy of your selfie face so other players see the real you. Private LIFEboard: goals, journal, trackers, reminders, money calculator inputs, letters to future you. Only your account can read it. Activity: messages you send, posts, comments, uploads, Global Match listings, reports, and basic sign-in records (time and IP address) kept by our login provider for security.

Safety checks on uploads

Before a photo or video is uploaded, your own browser checks it for nudity and sexual content and removes hidden location data (GPS/EXIF) from photos. Pictures that may break our rules are held privately for a moderator. We log each upload (who, when, file type, the safety-check scores) so moderators can act on reports.

What stays on your device

Guests keep everything in the browser on their own device. Fingerprint and face unlocks use your device's built-in passkey system; your biometric never leaves your device and we never receive it.

Who can see what

Your public profile and avatar are visible to other players. Direct messages are visible to you and the person you message. Room chat is visible to the room. Your LIFEboard is visible only to you. A small number of administrators can see account emails and reports to keep everyone safe.

How we use it

To run the world, show you to the people you choose to connect with, keep the community safe, and improve the site. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not show ads based on your LIFEboard.

Emails and messages from allofus

When you join we send a few account messages: a welcome, safety and security notices, and answers to anything you ask us. These are part of running your account. Updates, tips and the occasional digest go only to members who tick the email box when they join or turn it on later in the Control panel, and every one has a one-click unsubscribe. In-app notifications (for example when a Global Match lines up for you) are controlled in your settings. We never sell your email address or let advertisers message you.

Automated safety reading

Text in Global Match posts, public feed posts and comments is checked by automatic filters and by an AI safety reader, run for us by a service provider under contract that does not use it to train its models. It looks for drugs, weapons, sexual services, scams, exploitation, hate and other content the Community Rules ban. Anything it flags is held for a person to review, and serious cases follow the Law enforcement and NCMEC section below.

Adults only

allofus.one is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to use an account, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If someone tells us they are under 18, their account becomes read-only and is flagged for review; accounts of children under 13 are deleted except for anything we must keep for a legal or safety reason. If you believe a minor is using allofus.one, report the profile or contact us.

Law enforcement and NCMEC

We report apparent child sexual exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC CyberTipline) and may report other illegal activity to law enforcement. When we make a report, or receive valid legal process, we preserve and may share the related account information, content, messages, uploads and sign-in records. That information is kept for as long as the law or the investigation requires, even if the account is deleted.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, remove your selfie face, block people, and ask us to delete your account and data at any time (except what we must keep for a legal or safety reason, described above) by emailing info [at] eyetoad.com. We respond within 30 days.

Security

Data is stored with Supabase using row-level security, so the database itself only lets your account read your LIFEboard. No system is perfect, so please use a unique password.

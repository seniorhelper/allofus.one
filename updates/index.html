What's new on allofus.one

Every change that matters to members, in plain words, newest first. allofus.one is a free social network for adults 18+ with a feed that has no hidden algorithm. New here? Start with what allofus.one is.

November 22, 2026

Clearer pages and original names

  • A new About page explains what allofus.one is, who it is for and how the feed works, with answers to common questions.
  • This Updates page replaces the old list of build notes.
  • Moments are now called Glimmers (the old /moments/ link still works), Circles are now Audiences, and the marketing hub lives at /lift/.
  • Habit counts on your LIFEboard show your run of days with a sprout instead of a flame.
  • The Constellations page now explains every genre and the ten buy, sell, trade and free boards.
November 13, 2026

Smoother VR controls

  • In a VR headset the menu, wrist watch, your body and walking direction now follow your head correctly anywhere in the 3D world.
  • One button cycles four camera views, another opens the menu, and the laser pointer helps you hit what you aim at.
  • On laptops, the header no longer lets the tour button overlap the Invite button.
November 10–11, 2026

Private Rooms

  • Rooms can now be private or invite-only.
  • Open Rooms are visible to visitors who are not signed in, so you can look before you join.
November 7, 2026

Creators Corner opens, and a one-time age check

  • Creators Corner is open: a Tip button that goes to your own Venmo, PayPal or Cash App, so you keep 100% of what fans send. A person approves every creator.
  • Nine free creator tools run in your browser, with nothing uploaded.
  • Every member takes a one-time selfie age check. It runs on your own device and the picture never leaves it.
  • Push notifications are more reliable, and iPhone users get a short guide to adding allofus.one to the Home Screen.
  • You can react to comments.
November 3, 2026

Fixes you asked for

  • Deleting a Brand page now removes it completely, and deleting your account is sturdier.
  • The 3D house builder is easier to find and scrolls on small screens; drawing your own pool works again.
October 25, 2026

Constellations, Rooms and Global Match 2.0

  • Constellations are the new home for communities, drawn as a live star map. A person reviews each new one before it lights up.
  • Rooms bring live text chat, with a Room for every Constellation.
  • Global Match 2.0 adds a safety shield that screens every listing.
  • Our own names: Warm, Grin and Whoa reactions, World Window for NASA pictures and world news, and LIFEspark Lift for the marketing hub.
  • Videos are screened for safety, like photos already were.
October 23, 2026

Adults only, with real moderation

  • allofus.one is for adults 18+ only. Sign-up asks for a date of birth.
  • The dating features (Hearts and Crush) were removed.
  • Every post, comment, message and profile can be reported to a person. Child-safety reports go to NCMEC.
  • Audiences (then called Circles) let you share with Family, Inner Tribe, Professional or Local only, and every post shows why you are seeing it.
  • New: LIFEspark daily prompts, a guided tour, World Window, and deleting your own account from the Control panel.
October 19–22, 2026

Notifications and feed order

  • Push notifications on phones and laptops.
  • Choose your feed order: New (newest first, the default), Top or Hot. Each one says exactly how it works.
  • Polls show how you voted; scheduled posts stay hidden until their time.
  • VR: a new arrival scene and a wrist menu that opens where you look.
October 9, 2026

Launch

  • A cleaner header on laptops and a bottom bar on phones.
  • Invite friends with your own link, share any post or page, and switch between your profile and the pages you manage.
  • Download all your data as one file, or delete your account.
October 6, 2026

Communities, events, courses and the LIFEboard

  • Community spaces (now Constellations) with their own feed, members, events and courses.
  • Events with RSVPs and calendar files; courses with lessons and progress.
  • Free video calls for up to six people with one link, now called Unity Call.
  • Camera faces and backdrops for photos and 10-second clips, now called Glimmers.
  • The full LIFEboard opens right in the feed: goals, habits, a life wheel, a vision board and money tools.
October 4–5, 2026

The feed comes first

  • allofus.one opens on the feed. The 3D world moved to /world/ and opens only when you choose it.
  • Free Brand pages for businesses, run from your own profile.
  • The LIFEspark Lift marketing hub for businesses.
  • VR support for Meta Quest 3 and other WebXR headsets.
October 2–3, 2026

The first version

  • A free 3D world in the browser, the private LIFEboard and Global Match for "I need / I have" swaps of items and services.

Report and removal

Last updated November 23, 2026.

If something on allofus.one should not be here, tell us and we will act. You do not need an account to send a request, and it is free.

1. Intimate images shared without your consent

If an intimate photo or video of you, real or made with AI, was posted on allofus.one without your consent, you can ask us to remove it. We remove it within 48 hours of a valid request, and we make reasonable efforts to find and remove identical copies of it.

How to ask: email info [at] eyetoad.com with the subject Removal request, or call 1-800-481-8638. If you are a member, you can also use the Report button on the post, comment, message or profile.

Please include:

  • Where it is: the link, or the member name and what the post shows, so we can find it.
  • A short statement that the image shows you (or the person you are authorised to act for) and that it was shared without consent.
  • How to reach you: an email address or phone number.
  • Your signature. Typing your full name counts.

Do not send us the image itself. We keep your request private and never tell the person who posted it who reported it. Accounts that post this kind of content are closed.

2. Copyright (DMCA) notices

If your copyrighted work was posted here without permission, send a notice to info [at] eyetoad.com with the subject Copyright notice, or by mail to Eye To Ad Media, Attn: Copyright, Denver, Colorado. Include:

  • The work you own, and where the copy is on allofus.one (the link).
  • Your name, address, phone number and email.
  • A statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised by the owner, its agent or the law.
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that your notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

We remove or disable what a valid notice identifies and tell the member who posted it. That member may send a counter-notice with the same details and a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the removal was a mistake. We close the accounts of repeat infringers.

3. Child safety

allofus.one is for adults 18 and older. If you see anything that sexualises or endangers a child, report it to us right away with the Report button or at info [at] eyetoad.com, and you can also report directly to the NCMEC CyberTipline. We report apparent child sexual exploitation to NCMEC, as U.S. law requires. If a child is in immediate danger, call 911.

4. Anything else

For harassment, threats, scams, impersonation or anything that breaks the Community Rules, use the Report button or write to info [at] eyetoad.com. Reports go to a person, and you can block any member at any time.

Requests sent in bad faith, such as false claims made to silence someone, break our Terms of Use.

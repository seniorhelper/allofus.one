Our promise

Social media should work for you, not on you. These are the rules we build by, and you can hold us to them.

  • No ad trackingNo third-party ad pixels and no behavioral profile of you. The rare sponsored post is labeled and is never aimed at you by tracking.
  • We never sell your dataNot to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone.
  • No rage-bait rankingYour feed is newest first by default. If you pick another sort, we show you the exact formula. Every post can tell you why it is there.
  • You own your graphOne tap downloads your profile, posts, comments, connections and circles as an open JSON file. Leave any time and take it with you.
  • Audiences that never mixCircles let you share with Family, your Inner Tribe, work or neighbors without the others seeing it.
  • Adults only, and people firstallofus.one is 18+. Real people read every report, and harm to a child is reported to NCMEC and law enforcement.

Questions people ask

Is allofus.one free?

Yes. allofus.one is a free 3D world and social network. The LIFEboard tools, such as goals, the focus timer, trackers, reminders, the journal and the money calculators, are free and private.

Do I need a VR headset?

No. allofus.one runs in any web browser on a phone or laptop, and on WebXR headsets such as Quest 3 if you have one. There are three modes on one site: Flat, 3D and VR.

Does an algorithm decide what I see in my feed?

No. Your feed shows posts from the people you choose, newest first, in the order they happened. There is no ranking algorithm deciding what you deserve to see.

Is allofus.one family friendly?

It is clean and kind with no exceptions, and it is for adults 18 and older only: you confirm your date of birth when you join, there is a language filter, every post, message and profile can be reported or blocked, and a real person reviews every report.

Do you sell ads against my personal life?

No. We don't sell ads against your life, and we never will. You control your feed, your profile, your home, your LIFEboard and who sees what.

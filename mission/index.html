Dedicated to Stephanie Wennstedt

✌️

My mom. She passed in 2023, and she is loved and missed very much, every single day.

Everywhere she went, she threw the peace sign. Grocery store, parking lot, family photos, didn't matter. Her dream was simple and huge: world peace and love, man. This whole place carries that dream. The mall is shaped like a peace sign from the sky. The halo over every head, the hugs, the kindness rule, the "find the good": that's her.

What we believe

Yes, bad exists. But there is so, so much more good. Find the good.

Be the peace you seek. Be the love we need. Be the light, and chase away the dark, and we will be all right.

We all have value. We all are worthy. We all deserve life. We all deserve kindness and respect. That is not just our minimum standard, it is our compass.

Cruelty, in any form, is the opposite of our purpose.

Pick up those who have fallen and are hurting. Have an abundance? Find someone in need and bless them.

Small changes like these lead to a much better world, and a better home, for all of us.

Why a virtual world?

Because it is a safe place to practice. Here people from every country meet without borders, build things together, solve problems together and treat each other well. Those habits don't stay in the headset. Start solving problems together in here, and we get better at solving the real ones out there.

Questions people ask

Is allofus.one free?

Yes. allofus.one is a free 3D world and social network. The LIFEboard tools, such as goals, the focus timer, trackers, reminders, the journal and the money calculators, are free and private.

Do I need a VR headset?

No. allofus.one runs in any web browser on a phone or laptop, and on WebXR headsets such as Quest 3 if you have one. There are three modes on one site: Flat, 3D and VR.

Does an algorithm decide what I see in my feed?

No. Your feed shows posts from the people you choose, newest first, in the order they happened. There is no ranking algorithm deciding what you deserve to see.

Is allofus.one family friendly?

Yes, with no exceptions. Accounts are for ages 13 and up, there is a language filter and report and block tools, and teens are only matched with teens and adults only with adults in friend scan and voice chat.

Do you sell ads against my personal life?

No. We don't sell ads against your life, and we never will. You control your feed, your profile, your home, your LIFEboard and who sees what.

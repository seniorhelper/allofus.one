The Global Room. One room for everyone you need in it.

Your team, your class, your family, your whole group — up to 50 faces in one virtual space, a presentation wall you control, and a setting you pick. Flat in a browser, 3D on any screen, or VR on a Quest. Same room, however people join.

🛠️ Set up my room How it works

How it works

1 · Pick a settingConference room, park, beach, campfire or rooftop at night. Real sky, real light.
2 · Invite your peopleYour connections drop in with their own face and name. Anyone else gets a link. Nobody needs a headset.
3 · PresentThe wall shows your slides, a PDF, an HTML page or a video. You hold the clicker; everyone sees the same thing.
4 · Meet however you likeFlat, 3D or VR, in the same room at the same time. Faces for everyone, bodies for the ones in 3D and VR.

Honest status: rooms, invites, text, the presentation wall and 3D/VR spaces are live now. Live multi-person video is rolling out in stages; your room is ready for it the moment it arrives.

Questions

Is it free?Setting up a room, inviting your connections, text and the presentation wall are free. Live video is rolling out in stages.
Do people need a headset?No. Flat works in any browser; 3D on a laptop or phone; VR on a Quest. Everyone is in the same room.
Can I present slides?Yes: a link to slides, a PDF, an HTML page or a video. You control what the wall shows.

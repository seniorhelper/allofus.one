Accessibility Statement

Last updated October 2, 2026.

allofus.one should work for all of us. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA for everything outside the 3D view, and we keep improving the 3D world itself.

What works today

Keyboard controls for walking (WASD or arrows) and for every menu, dialog and LIFEboard tool. Every place is reachable by search and teleport, so you never have to navigate the 3D world by sight. Light, high-contrast panels for all reading and forms. Buttons have text labels, not just icons. Reduced-motion settings in your device tone down animation. Touch controls on phones and tablets. Lumi, the scripted guide, answers in plain text and can be tucked away.

Known limits

Parts of the 3D world are visual by nature. Some decorative 3D details have no text alternative. Weather effects and fireworks are optional and only change your own view.

Tell us about a barrier

If something does not work for you, email info [at] eyetoad.com or call 1-800-481-8638. Tell us the page, what you were trying to do and the device or assistive technology you use. We aim to reply within 5 business days.

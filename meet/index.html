Unity Call

Comm HubFeed
Unity Call: free video calls. Start one, send the link, up to six people. Runs browser to browser — we never see or store your video. Start one from any conversation in your Comm Hub and the invite lands right in the thread.

Unity Calls are peer-to-peer, so each person's browser talks directly to the others; six is the comfortable limit. If someone cannot connect (strict office networks), try a phone hotspot. For bigger groups use the Global Room.

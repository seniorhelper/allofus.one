How to download your social media data before you leave a platform

Step-by-step: download your data from Instagram, Facebook, X and YouTube before you leave, what each export includes, and how to keep it safe.

Short answer

Every major platform lets you export a copy of your data: Instagram and Facebook through "Export your information" in Meta's account settings, X through "Your X data", and YouTube through Google Takeout. Request the export first, wait for the email, download and check the file, and only then delete the account.

Thinking about leaving a platform, or just want a backup of years of photos and posts? Download first, decide later. The steps below come from each platform's own help pages, opened on November 22, 2026. Menu names change often, so if a label looks different, follow the help page linked in each section.

Where is most people's data?

For most US adults, on a handful of big platforms. Pew Research Center's 2025 survey of 5,022 adults found YouTube and Facebook far ahead of everything else (Pew Research Center, "Americans' Social Media Use 2025", November 20, 2025).

Before you start: three rules

  1. Use a computer if you can. Exports are large ZIP files and are easier to save and open on a laptop.
  2. Choose JSON if you might move the data somewhere else, and HTML if you just want to read it in a browser.
  3. Don't delete anything until you've opened the download and checked your photos and posts are there.

How do you download your Instagram data?

From the Instagram Help Center (Meta is moving people from "Accounts Center" to "Meta Account", so you may see either name):

  1. Open Settings, then Meta Account (or Accounts Center).
  2. Choose Your information and permissions, then Export your information, then Create export.
  3. Pick the profile, then Export to device. Choose what to include, the date range, the format (HTML or JSON) and media quality.
  4. Tap Start export, wait for the email, and download the file.

How do you download your Facebook data?

Facebook uses the same Meta settings (Facebook Help Center: export a copy of your information):

  1. Click your profile picture, then Settings & privacy, then Settings.
  2. Choose Meta Account, then Your information and permissions, then Export your information.
  3. Click Create export, pick your Facebook profile, and choose Export to device.
  4. Choose the information, date range, format and media quality, then Start export.

If you have more than one Facebook profile, export each one. Facebook's help page notes that some information, including ads information, only comes with the export of your main profile.

How do you download your X (Twitter) archive?

From the X Help Center (How to download your X archive):

  1. Go to Settings and privacy, then Account.
  2. Under Data and permissions, choose Your X data and verify it's you with the code X sends to your email or phone.
  3. Under Download your data, choose Request data.
  4. X emails you (or sends a push notification) when it's ready, which can take a few days. Download the ZIP from the same settings page while signed in.

How do you download your YouTube and Google data?

Google Takeout exports YouTube videos along with other Google data (Google Account Help: download your data). Go to the Google Takeout page; the Google products you use are selected for you, so untick the ones you don't want. Choose the file type (ZIP opens on almost any computer) and the maximum archive size, then create the export. You get an email with a link when it's done. Large libraries are split into several files.

What should you do with the files?

  • Keep two copies: one on your computer and one on an external drive or a cloud folder you control.
  • Treat them as private. Exports include messages and contact details. Don't email them or upload them anywhere you don't trust.
  • Write down what's missing. If photos or chats you care about aren't in the export, save them by hand before you delete the account.

How does exporting work on allofus.one?

On your data page you can download your profile, posts, comments, reactions, connections and audiences as one open JSON file, any time, straight from the page. You can also make an identity key in your browser and sign the export with it, so anyone can check the file is yours without asking our servers (mission page). If you're bringing a group over from somewhere else, Constellations give it a home with its own feed and live Room.

Questions people ask

Does downloading my data delete my account?

No. An export is a copy. Your account and everything in it stay as they are until you delete the account separately, and you should only do that after checking the download.

How long does a data export take?

It depends on the platform and how much you have posted. X says its archive can take a few days, and large photo or video libraries take longer on every platform.

Is JSON or HTML better?

HTML is easier to read in a browser. JSON is structured data that other apps and tools can import. If the platform allows it, download both.

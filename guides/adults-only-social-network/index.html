An adults-only social network: how age checks and reports work

How an 18+ social network keeps minors out: a private date of birth, a one-time on-device selfie age check, human-read reports, and the honest limits.

"Adults only" is easy to write in a footer. What matters is what happens at the door, and what happens when someone breaks the rules anyway. This guide describes exactly how allofus.one handles both today, where the rules are written down, and what no age check can promise. It describes only what is live on the site as of November 22, 2026.

What does "adults only" mean on allofus.one?

Our community rules say every account is for one adult, 18 or older. There is no dating, matchmaking or "crush" feature. The site is clean and kind with no exceptions: no nudity or sexual content of any kind, no profanity, no bullying, harassment, threats or hate, and no scams or spam.

One part of allofus.one is open to everyone: the 3D World can be explored by anyone. Chat, voice, messages and connections stay adults only, so a visitor who isn't a signed-in adult never talks with other people there.

How does the age check work?

There are two steps: a date of birth when you sign up, and a one-time selfie age check after you sign in.

Step one: date of birth at sign-up

  1. Date of birth. The sign-up form asks for your date of birth. It is private: it is never shown on your profile.
  2. A clear confirmation. You also tick a box to confirm you are 18 or older.
  3. Under 18 means no account. If the date of birth shows someone is under 18, no account is created. They're thanked for being honest and pointed to the all-ages 3D World instead.
  4. Older accounts too. Accounts made before the date-of-birth step are asked to confirm it before they can talk with people.

Step two: a one-time selfie age check, on your device

After you sign in, allofus.one asks every member (new and existing) to do a quick selfie age check, once. Here is exactly what it does:

  1. It runs in your browser. When you tap Start, your browser downloads a small open-source face model (about 2 MB, once) from allofus.one itself and asks to use your camera.
  2. Your picture never leaves your device. Your phone or computer looks at your face for a few seconds and estimates your age right there. The camera picture is never uploaded and never saved, and the camera turns off when the check ends.
  3. Only the number is kept. allofus.one saves the estimated age, the result, how many readings were taken and the date. Nothing else from the check is stored.
  4. What the result means. An estimate of 25 or older: you're done and won't be asked again. 18 to 24: you're let in, and the account is flagged so a person can double-check later. Under 18: posting, comments, messages and uploads are paused until a person reviews the account.

If the camera can't see your face clearly, you can try again in better light. If you have no camera, the check screen gives a phone number to call for help. The privacy policy describes the same check under "Adults only".

If an existing member tells us they are under 18, the account becomes read-only and is flagged for review. It can never message, connect with or call anyone. Accounts of children under 13 are deleted, except for anything we must keep for a legal or safety reason (privacy policy).

What can you report, and how?

Every post, comment, message, picture and profile has a Report option in its ⋯ menu. You choose a reason and can add a short note. You need to be signed in to report, and the person you report is not told who reported them. The reasons on the report form are:

What happens after you report something?

  • A person reads it. Reports go to a human moderation queue. A person reviews every report.
  • Some things are hidden at once. Content that several members report, and any child-safety report, is hidden right away until it's reviewed. A profile reported as under 18 is limited right away while we check.
  • Then a decision. Breaking the rules can get content removed and accounts banned.
  • Zero tolerance. Child sexual abuse material, grooming or trying to contact a minor, sextortion, trafficking, and selling illegal goods mean an immediate, permanent ban. We report child exploitation to NCMEC and other crimes to law enforcement, and preserve the evidence for them.

What does blocking do?

You can block anyone from their profile. Blocked people cannot message, call or connect with you. Blocking is instant and doesn't need a moderator.

What else keeps the space adult and clean?

  • New accounts wait for uploads. New accounts can post text right away; photo, video and file uploads unlock after 72 hours.
  • Uploads are checked. Before a photo or video uploads, your own browser checks it for nudity and sexual content and strips hidden location data from photos. Pictures that may break the rules are held privately for a moderator.
  • Text is checked too. Chat has a language filter. Public feed posts, comments and Global Match listings are also checked by automatic filters and an AI safety reader, and anything flagged is held for a person to review.

What can't an age check do?

Be honest about the limits. A date of birth is something a person types in, and a person can type in a false one. A selfie age estimate is a first filter, not proof of age: it can be off by a few years either way, which is why anyone estimated under 25 can be checked by a person, and why an under-18 estimate only pauses an account until a person reviews it instead of closing it. That's also why the rules don't stop at the door: reports of anyone who seems under 18 limit their account straight away, and a person looks at every report. If you run or join any adults-only network, look for the same three things: a check at the door, a fast way to report, and a human who reads the reports.

Questions people ask

Is my date of birth shown on my profile?

No. allofus.one uses your date of birth only to keep the site adults-only. It is private and never shown on your profile.

Does allofus.one keep my selfie from the age check?

No. The age check runs on your own device. The camera picture is never uploaded or saved; only the estimated age, the result, the number of readings and the date are kept.

Will the person I report know it was me?

No. The report form says the person you report is not told who reported them.

Can teenagers use any part of allofus.one?

Accounts are for adults 18 and older only. The 3D World is open to everyone to explore, but chat, voice, messages and connections stay adults-only.

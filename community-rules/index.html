Community Rules

Last updated October 2, 2026.

allofus.one is a world for all of us: kids with their families, grandparents, teens, founders and dreamers. Our mission is simple. Push for peace, love, collaboration, new friendships and growth. Hugs are plentiful and free.

Always welcome

Kindness. Curiosity. Helping someone find their way. Sharing your work, your art and your causes. Disagreeing politely. Celebrating other people's wins. Sending hugs.

Never allowed

allofus.one is family friendly with no exceptions. That means no adult or sexual content of any kind, no profanity or crude language, no bullying, harassment, threats or hate, no scams, spam or selling people things they did not ask for, no sharing anyone's private information, no impersonation, and nothing illegal. Chat has a language filter, and it is a backstop, not a permission slip.

Kids and teens

Accounts are for people 13 and older. Kids under 13 are welcome to explore the world with a parent or guardian but may not create an account, chat or use friend scan. Friend scan only ever matches teens with teens and adults with adults. Adults may not seek out minors for any reason.

Private spaces

Every LIFEboard is private to its owner. Homes can be private inside. Model homes like No. 1 Unity Road are open to tour. Never try to get into someone else's account, home or LIFEboard.

Reports, blocks and bans

Tap anyone's avatar to 🚩 Report or 🚫 Block them. A real person reviews reports. Breaking these rules can get content removed and accounts banned, and anything illegal can be reported to the authorities. If someone is in danger, call 911 (in the U.S.) or your local emergency number first.

Using the icon

Don't change the markNo recolouring, stretching, rotating, outlines, shadows or effects. Use the colour, dark or white version as it comes.
Minimum size16px for the icon (24px or larger is best). Keep the follow badge at least 32px tall so the words stay readable.
Clear spaceLeave space around the icon of at least a quarter of its width. Don't let other logos or text touch it.
Link to your pageAlways link it to your own allofus.one page or profile, so the icon takes people straight to you.
Next to your other iconsMatch the size of your other social icons. The one-colour versions sit nicely in a row of one-colour icons.
It's not an endorsementThe icon says you're on allofus.one. Please don't use it to suggest allofus.one sponsors or approves your business.

Questions

Where do I find my page address?

Open your Brand page on allofus.one and copy the link from your browser. It looks like allofus.one/?page=your-business. For a personal profile, it's allofus.one/?u=yourname. Paste either one above.

Does the badge track my visitors?

No. The code is a plain link and an image. There are no scripts, no cookies and no tracking pixels.

My email signature won't show the icon.

Some email apps block SVG images. Pick PNG (email signatures) above and copy the code again.

# Weekly posting rhythm for allofus.one (founder account)

*2026-10-23. A small network feels alive when the same voice shows up at the same times. Ten minutes a day, two sessions. Pull posts from `seed-posts.md`, then keep writing in the same voice.*

## The rule of thumb

- **Two posts a day, max.** One in the morning (Denver, 7-8 am), one in the evening (6-8 pm). More than that from one account reads as noise.
- **Reply to every comment within the day.** On a small network a reply is worth more than a post.
- **One question a day.** Questions get answers; statements get scrolls.
- **One circle post a week** so Circles are seen in use, not just described.
- **Never post the same seed twice.** Tick them off in the table at the bottom.

| **Saturday** | Photo from real life, one sentence (seed 2 as the model) | Circle post to Inner Tribe (seed 8) | Light day. One post is fine. |
| **Sunday** | Weekly digest goes out at 8 am Denver (automatic once pg_cron is on) | "What should the feed show when friends are quiet?" style reflection (seed 25) | Plan next week's two business tips |

## Monthly

- **Week 1:** welcome + orientation heavy (seeds 1-5). Pin the tour post.
- **Week 2:** Circles week (seeds 6-8) plus one how-to a day.
- **Week 3:** business week: Brand pages, listings, press release, Speed to Leads (seeds 15-20). Invite three local businesses by name (privately).
- **Week 4:** data and promise week (seeds 26-28) plus a kindness thread.
- **Last day of the month:** post one honest number (members, posts, or Brand pages) and one thing that did not work. Pull the number from the SQL in MASTER-GAMEPLAN §10; never guess.

## Where content comes from when you are empty

1. **Discover** (/?v=discover): NASA, Wikipedia featured, world news, with credit attached. One share a week is plenty.
2. **LIFEspark** (/?v=spark): 125 prompts rotate daily; answer the day's prompt in one line and post it.
3. **Your own sites' news**: new blog posts on eyetoad.com or aispeedtoleads.com, shared as a link with one sentence of why it matters to members (link policy: `rel="sponsored"` is automatic for own sites in the guide; in a post it is just a link).
4. **Members' posts**: reposting a member's good post with a one-line why is better than any seed.
5. **Pixabay**: only once a free API key is added (gameplan §14); even then, share the Pixabay page link and credit, never the image file.

## Do not

- Do not post for a client business from the founder account, and never mention Bighorn Painting.
- Do not quote books, songs or famous people. Original words only; the originality check runs before every push.
- Do not post numbers you have not pulled from SQL that day.
- Do not reply to a lead or a complaint publicly; move it to DMs or a call.

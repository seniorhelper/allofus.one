## Circles

6. **How-to** · Public
   Circles in sixty seconds: open Circles, add a few people to Family, a few to Inner Tribe, a few to Professional. Next time you post, pick "Who sees this". A circle post never leaves that circle, and nobody is told which circles they are in.

## Business tips (Brand pages and Grow)

15. **Business tip** · Public
    If you run a business, your Brand page here is free and it is not a toy: logo, cover, services, FAQ, hours, call and website buttons, and schema markup that search engines actually read. Set it up from your profile, then post from it.

16. **Business tip** · Public
    The cheapest marketing move most small businesses skip: a free listing in a real directory. Grow your business (/?v=grow) sends you to two of ours with your details ready to paste. Ten minutes, done for good.

17. **Business tip** · Public
    A press release is not just for big companies. "Local painter opens second crew" is news to your town. The Grow page drafts one from your Brand page; you edit it and publish. Three sentences and a phone number beat a perfect release never sent.

18. **Business tip** · Public
    Speed beats polish. The business that replies to a web inquiry in one minute usually wins it; the one that replies tomorrow usually does not. If you cannot answer in a minute, AI Speed to Leads will, only for people who asked to be contacted, and it hands them to you.

19. **Question** · Public
    Business owners: what is the one question every new customer asks you? Post it below. I will turn the best ones into Brand page FAQ examples.

20. **Business tip** · Public
    A Space is a free home for your customers: early access, how-tos, a monthly call. Skool and Circle charge for this. Here it comes with the account. Start one with five people and a weekly post.

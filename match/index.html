Why it works when search doesn't

Search makes you do the work and only shows what's already listed. Global Match works both directions, keeps looking after you close the tab, and pings you in your Comm Hub when the other half appears, next door or across the world. Every match carries a kindness score from the people who matched before, so you know who you're meeting.

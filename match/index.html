The Match Shield

A matcher this open has to be the safest place on the internet to ask for help. Every post passes four layers, and the people who try to misuse it lose their accounts.

LAYER 1 · INSTANTFilters as you type

Catches drug, weapon, sex-service, fraud and hate words, including misspellings, spacing tricks, look-alike letters and emoji codes.

LAYER 2 · DATABASEA lock nobody can skip

The same rules run inside the database on every post. A blocked post never goes public and is recorded as a strike.

LAYER 3 · AIAn AI safety reader

Every new post is read for meaning within seconds: scams, trafficking signs, someone in danger. Anything unsure waits for a person.

LAYER 4 · 7AM + 7PMTwice-daily sweep

Morning and evening, the whole day's posts and comments are read again, and anything flagged goes to a moderator.

Strike 1Post removed and a warning shown.
Strike 2Second warning. Moderators can see the history.
Strike 3Posting paused and the account goes to a moderator. Serious cases are banned at once and reported.

Never on Global Match: drugs or prescriptions, guns or ammo, sexual services or dating, stolen or fake goods, hacking, hate or violence. Do not post or search for them.

Plus the basics: adults 18+ only, new accounts post 3 a day, messages stay inside allofus, and every card has a Report button read by a real person. Meet in public, never pay strangers up front, and never share codes.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Posting, matching and messaging are free, and nothing is ranked for money.

Who can see my post?

Everyone can read open posts. Your city is shown if you add it; never add your street address. Talk details in allofus messages.

What does "Tidy with AI" do?

It rewrites your words into a clear title, picks the kind and keywords, and checks it for safety. You see the result before anything is posted. Nothing is posted until you press Post.

Does money change hands here?

No. allofus never holds money for a match. If you sell something, you settle it in person, safely. Never pay a stranger up front, and never share a verification code.

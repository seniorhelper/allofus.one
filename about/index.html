About allofus.one

allofus.one is a free social network for adults 18+. Your feed shows posts newest first by default, with no hidden algorithm deciding what you see. You get your own page, free Brand pages for a business, communities called Constellations, live chat Rooms, free video calls and a private LIFEboard for goals and habits. A 3D world, which also works in VR headsets, is there when you want it. Everything runs in your web browser on one account.

Join free

Who it is for

allofus.one is for adults who want to keep up with real people without a feed that is tuned to keep them scrolling. Members are friends and families, neighbors, clubs and small businesses. It is built in Denver, Colorado, and open to the whole world. Everyone must be 18 or older: sign-up asks for a date of birth, and every member takes a one-time selfie age check that runs on their own device, so the picture never leaves it.

How the feed works

You choose the order. New is the default: newest first, with no ranking at all. Top puts the posts with the most reactions and comments first. Hot uses the published Reddit formula, so a fresh post with a few reactions can beat an old post with many. Each option says exactly how it works on screen. With Rings you can share a post with only your Family, Inner Tribe, Professional or Local people, and every post can tell you why you are seeing it.

What you can do

Constellations

Communities with their own feed, live Room and guestbook, in twelve genres, plus ten buy, sell, trade and free boards.

Rooms

Live text chat. Drop in, see who is here and message people you meet.

Global Match

Write one line about what you need or what you have, and find the other half.

Unity Call

Free video calls for up to six people with one link. Guests need no account.

Glimmers

Camera faces and backdrops for a photo or a 10-second clip, made on your device.

3D world and VR

Walk a 3D world with your avatar, meet people and build a home. Works in WebXR headsets.

Creators Corner

A Tip button that opens your own Venmo, PayPal or Cash App, so you keep 100%.

LIFEspark Lift

The marketing hub for businesses: a free ad maker, a campaign builder and paid packages.

The rules, in short

Adults 18+ only. Be kind and keep it clean. Slurs and harassment are blocked on posts, comments and Rooms. You can block anyone, and every post, comment, message and profile can be reported to a person on our moderation team. Child-safety reports go to NCMEC. Ads are rare, say Sponsored, are reviewed by a person and can be turned off. Read the full community rules, terms and privacy policy.

Questions people ask

What is allofus.one?

allofus.one is a free social network for adults 18+, built in Denver, Colorado. It has a feed that shows posts newest first by default with no hidden algorithm, your own page, free Brand pages for businesses, communities called Constellations, live chat Rooms, free Unity Call video and a private LIFEboard for goals, habits and money. An optional 3D world also works in VR headsets. It all runs in a web browser on one account.

Who can join allofus.one?

Adults 18 and older, anywhere in the world. Sign-up asks for your date of birth, and every member takes a one-time selfie age check. The check runs on your own device and the picture never leaves it. If an account looks like it belongs to someone under 18, it is held so it cannot post, comment, message or upload until a person on our team reviews it.

How does the allofus.one feed decide what I see?

You decide. The default order is New: newest first, with no ranking at all. You can switch to Top, which puts the posts with the most reactions and comments first, or Hot, which uses the published Reddit formula so fresh posts rise. Each choice explains itself on screen. With Rings you can share a post with only your Family, Inner Tribe, Professional or Local people, and every post can tell you why it is there.

Is allofus.one free?

Yes. Joining, posting, your page, Brand pages, Constellations, Rooms, Unity Call video, Global Match, Glimmers, the LIFEboard and the 3D world are all free. The paid parts are for businesses: LIFEspark Lift marketing packages and ad placements. Creators Corner tips go straight to the creator's own Venmo, PayPal or Cash App; allofus.one never holds that money and takes no cut.

How does allofus.one keep people safe?

The site is adults-only. Slurs and harassment are blocked on posts, comments and Rooms, and photos and videos are screened before they are shared. You can block anyone, and every post, comment, message and profile has a report button that goes to a person on our moderation team. Child-safety reports are sent to NCMEC. Ads are labeled Sponsored, reviewed by a person and never targeted by tracking.

Do I need an app or a VR headset?

No. allofus.one runs in the web browser on a phone, tablet or computer, and you can add it to your home screen. The feed, Constellations, Rooms and the LIFEboard are all flat pages. The 3D world is optional and opens only when you choose it. If you have a WebXR headset, such as a Meta Quest 3, you can step into the same world in VR.

allofus.one is a free 3D world you walk into from any browser. Make an avatar of yourself, walk the life paths you care about, and meet the people walking them too. Help each other, plan your life, and build a home on land that is free for everyone.

🌍 Step into the world

How it works

Six steps from stranger to neighbor.

No feed run by a secret algorithm. No hunting for people in a list. You find each other the way people always have: by being in the same place, going the same direction.

  1. 🧑

    Make your avatar

    Pick a character, dress it up, and add your real face with a selfie if you like. The photo stays on your device.

  2. 🟢

    Set your status ring

    A glowing ring at your feet tells everyone if you are home, hanging out, working or exploring, or if you'd rather not be disturbed.

  3. 🛤️

    Pick your life paths

    Business, Learning, Wellness, Adventure, Causes, Creativity or Explore. Your avatar stands on your path so others can find you.

  4. 👋

    Wave, connect, collaborate

    Wave at anyone. Send a connect request. When they say yes, chat unlocks. Request a call, meeting or VR visit.

  5. 🏠

    Build your Lifeboard

    Your home dashboard: life-balance wheel, goal stacker, vision board, habits, projects, booking link, and buttons you design.

  6. 🏡

    Claim land, build home

    Every road goes on forever, and there are open lots along every one. Claim yours and pick a style: Treehouse, Space Villa, Beach Villa and more.

Global Match

Someone, somewhere, needs exactly what you have.

Post an I Have or an I Need. Items and services: a couch, tools, coding lessons, help moving, a wall to paint a mural on. When they line up, both of you hear about it.

Global Match is for items and services only. No money changes hands on allofus.one.

Your feed, your rules

Newest first, from the people you choose.

You decide who can post to your feed (connections, path-mates or anyone) and what you want to see: updates, wins, asks or offers. Nothing is boosted. Nothing is buried.

Why this exists

Built for the long road toward each other.

More ways to talk than ever. Fewer ways to find each other. So here is a place where you can see who is walking the same road you are.

Questions

Good questions, quick answers.

What is in the Lifeboard?

Your private life dashboard: Today (a daily affirmation that never repeats on a loop, top 3 priorities and a streak), Life Dash with every tool on one screen, goals, a vision board, a focus timer, trackers with real charts, reminders you can add to your phone calendar, a journal, a sealed letter to future you, money calculators (budget, debt payoff, savings, compound growth, emergency fund), hugs and a halo sign. All free, private to your account.

Can I visit Zach's house?

Yes. No. 1 Unity Road is the allofus.one model home and anyone can walk in: twin waterfalls, gallery halls, a 360 Dream Room built from a real photo, animated shag carpet, neon light switches, a fireplace you can change, an elevator to the office and a roof-deck pool you can swim in, plus a backyard rocket that launches when you walk up. Lifeboards always stay private.

Is allofus.one family friendly?

Always. No adult content or language of any kind, a chat language filter, report and block on every profile, accounts for ages 13 and up, and friend scan only matches teens with teens and adults with adults. Read the Community Rules at allofus.one/community-rules/.

How do hugs work?

Tap 🤗 on anyone to send a burst of hearts their way, and they get a hug notice wherever they are in the world. You can also email a hug card to anyone, even if they are not on allofus.one yet.

Can I design my own house?

Yes. Claim free land, then open ✨ More → 🏗️ Home builder: pick a shape, floors, walls, a roof type and material (slate, aluminum, clay, cedar, copper, solar or a living roof), draw your own pool with fish, add gardens, solar, wind, a sign and a mailbox, or build anything with Lifeblocks. You see it in 3D as you go.

Can people visit my home from outside the world?

Yes. Every home has a street address and a unique address code. Share your link from the builder or the 📒 Directory and it walks visitors right to your door.

Is there voice chat?

Yes. Real peer-to-peer voice rooms: people near you, the plaza, the conference hall and more. Teens only hear teens and adults only hear adults, and nothing is recorded.

Can I give a presentation?

Yes. The Conference Center has 72 seats and a 26 meter screen. Upload or link a PDF, PowerPoint, Word file, MP3, video, image, YouTube video or web page and everyone in the hall sees it live.

Is it free?

Yes. Your avatar, the world, connecting, chat, Global Match, your Lifeboard and your land are free.

Do I need a VR headset?

No. It runs in the browser on phones, tablets and laptops. On a headset like Meta Quest, open allofus.one in the headset browser and tap Enter VR.

How do I move around?

On a laptop: WASD or the arrow keys, Shift to run, drag to look, click anything that glows. On a phone: the stick on the left to move, drag to look, tap the ground to walk there. Q dives in deep water, H opens your Lifeboard.

Who is Lumi?

The glowing orb on the right edge of the world. Lumi is a scripted guide, not AI: ask how things work or say "take me to the beach."

What is Global Match for?

Items and services: things you have that someone needs, and things you need that someone has. No money changes hands on allofus.one. Meet in public and never send money to strangers.

Who are the people standing on the paths?

Some are demo residents that show how profiles work (their cards say so). As people join, real profiles take their places along the paths they choose.

Is my data private?

Your selfie stays on your device. Your Lifeboard is private to you. You choose who can message you, who can post to your feed, and whom to block.
